HOW TO PLAY

Put the internet in order.

Internet Timeline is a daily chronological puzzle. You are shown five moments from internet, technology and digital culture history, one at a time.

1. Read the event

You might see a product launch, social platform, web milestone, gaming moment or AI breakthrough.

2. Choose where it belongs

Tap the gap where you think the event belongs relative to the events already on the timeline.

3. Lock it in

Your placement is scored immediately, then the correct year is revealed and the event joins the timeline.

4. Finish five rounds

Your final score is out of 5. The same daily puzzle is used for every player, so scores are directly comparable.
