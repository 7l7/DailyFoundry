Replay every Internet Timeline.

Internet Timeline is a daily history game about the web and digital culture. Each puzzle gives you five events to place in chronological order. Past puzzles stay playable, so you can catch up or replay them whenever you want.

Open the playable archive →

What is in the archive?

Puzzles cover milestones from early networking and the World Wide Web to search, social media, smartphones, gaming, streaming, cryptocurrency and generative AI. Every daily puzzle uses the same set of events for every player.

Can I replay a puzzle?

Yes. Open the playable archive, choose a puzzle number and replay it as many times as you want. Your replay best score is stored locally in your browser.
